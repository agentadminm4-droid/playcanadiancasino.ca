What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — Required for basic site functionality (e.g., navigation, secure areas). These cannot be disabled without affecting site performance.
  • Analytics cookies — Help us understand how visitors use our site via tools like Google Analytics. Data is anonymous and aggregated.
  • Affiliate tracking cookies — Used to track when you click our links to casino sites. This helps us measure referral commissions.
  • Preference cookies — Remember your settings (e.g., language, region) so you don't need to re-set them.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete all or specific cookies
  • Block cookies from all or certain websites
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Clear all cookies when you close your browser

Note: Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use, such as Google Analytics and affiliate tracking tools. These third parties may combine the data they collect with other information they've collected across other websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties.
