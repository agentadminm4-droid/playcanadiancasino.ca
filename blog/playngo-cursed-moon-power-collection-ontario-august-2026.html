How Cursed Moon Power Collection Plays

The base game is built on a 5x4 reel layout with way wins — adjacent matching symbols count from left to right regardless of payline position, which gives the title a higher hit density than traditional fixed-line slots. The visual identity leans hard into blood-red moonlight, gothic stone architecture, and a deep purple atmosphere; this is not a light-hearted cartoon slot.

The headline mechanic is coin collection, structured the way Play'n GO has favoured across its 2026 catalogue:

  • Coin symbols land exclusively on reels 2, 3, and 4 (the middle three reels), each carrying a random value from a fixed bet multiplier pool.
  • Slay Collector symbols land exclusively on reels 1 and 5 (the outer reels). When a Slay Collector hits on the same spin as a Coin, the Coin's value is collected and paid out.
  • Instant Win symbols can also appear and are collected by Slay Collectors alongside Coins.

Coin-and-Collector architecture is a known quantity from prior Play'n GO releases, but the twist here is that the Collector symbol is thematic: Slay Collectors are Dante's weapons, Coins are the spoils. The story and the gameplay loop reinforce each other.

The Soul Fire Multiplier

The Soul Fire Multiplier is the title's signature math driver. Each Slay Collector that lands adds to a multiplier meter that persists across the base game and into the Free Spins round. The multiplier increases with every successful collection and resets only when the bonus round ends, which means a long Free Spins run with frequent Slay Collector landings can push the multiplier into territory that materially changes the payout profile.

The Soul Fire Multiplier is the explicit "Power Collection" mechanic that gives the sub-series its name — the multiplier is the power, the collection is the trigger, and the thematic justification ("Dante grows stronger as he hunts") is baked into the symbol vocabulary. Players who have run Holy Moo! Extreme Power will recognise the architecture; Cursed Moon Power Collection layers it into a darker, more dramatic visual context.

Free Spins Progression: Five Levels

Three or more Scatter symbols trigger the Free Spins round, and the feature is structured around a five-level progression system. Higher levels unlock more Slay Collector symbols on the reels, higher starting Soul Fire Multiplier values, and additional Coins on the grid per spin.

The progression mechanic is the title's retention hook: rather than a single static bonus round, players are chasing the next level up, which extends session length and increases the value of the Free Spins trigger over the long run. Five levels is enough to feel like a meaningful climb without dragging the bonus round out into a grind.
