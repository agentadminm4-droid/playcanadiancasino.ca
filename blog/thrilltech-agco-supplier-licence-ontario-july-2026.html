ThrillTech Secures AGCO Supplier Licence to Bring Side-Bet Jackpot Tech to Ontario

July 15, 2026 • 7 min read • By PlayCanadianCasino Team

Side-bet jackpot specialist ThrillTech has secured a gaming-related supplier licence from the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario (AGCO), clearing the way for its ThrillPots product to go live with regulated Ontario operators. The licence, confirmed in a company press release dated July 15, 2026, brings ThrillTech into a growing roster of B2B suppliers cleared to provide game-side technology to Ontario's regulated iGaming market.

According to the supplier's announcement, the AGCO registration allows ThrillTech's side-bet jackpot engine to integrate with operator platforms serving Ontario-licensed players. ThrillTech is already licensed in the United Kingdom, Sweden, the Netherlands, Romania, Malta, Gibraltar, Brazil, and Peru; Ontario becomes the company's tenth regulated market and its second in North America after a recent US expansion.

ThrillTech's flagship product, ThrillPots, is a player-funded progressive jackpot layer that sits on top of an operator's existing slot lobby. Players opt in for a small side stake and become eligible for tiered jackpots (Mini, Minor, Major, Grand) without leaving the base game. The model has become increasingly common across regulated European markets as a player-retention and re-engagement tool, and ThrillTech's entry into Ontario marks the first side-bet jackpot supplier to publicly announce AGCO registration in 2026.

Key Stats: ThrillTech's Ontario Entry

  • Licence type: AGCO gaming-related supplier registration (B2B), not an operator licence
  • Product: ThrillPots side-bet jackpot engine, four-tier progressive (Mini, Minor, Major, Grand)
  • Existing markets: UK, Sweden, Netherlands, Romania, Malta, Gibraltar, Brazil, Peru, plus US states
  • 2026 supplier registrations in Ontario: ThrillTech joins Evoplay, Random State, Fennica Gaming, Playnetic, Gaming Corps, ELK Studios, ODDSworks, St8, TaDa Gaming, and edict egaming among recently approved suppliers
  • Operator model: Opt-in side-stake; player-funded jackpots; integrates into existing slot lobbies without replacing base games

What the AGCO Supplier Licence Covers

The AGCO distinguishes between operator licences (which allow a company to run a gaming site) and gaming-related supplier registrations (which allow a B2B vendor to provide technology, content, or services to those operators). ThrillTech falls into the second category. Its registration permits the company to contract with any of the 50+ AGCO-licensed operators and platforms that serve Ontario players, including BetMGM, Caesars, FanDuel, DraftKings, bet365, theScore Bet, and the OLG-operated channel.

For Ontario players, the practical effect will be visible at the slot-lobby level: when ThrillPots is integrated with an operator, individual slot games will display an opt-in ThrillPots button that lets players place a small side wager in exchange for eligibility in the operator's jackpot pool. None of the base-game math changes; the side bet is additive.

Why Side-Bet Jackpots Are Different From In-Game Jackpots

Most progressive jackpots in Ontario slots today are built into the game itself (e.g. a Mega Moolah-style wheel or a fixed-pool feature). ThrillPots is a network-agnostic layer that any slot provider can plug into. From a player perspective, the difference is that the jackpot pool is funded by opt-in side stakes across the entire operator lobby rather than by the base-game RTP of a single title. From a regulator's perspective, the side-bet mechanic requires its own RTG and game-event approvals in Ontario, which is why an AGCO supplier registration is needed in addition to any per-operator integration.

This dual-approval structure is one reason side-bet jackpot suppliers have been a relatively late entrant into the Ontario market compared with traditional slot studios. The first wave of suppliers that secured AGCO registration in 2024-2025 focused on slot content rather than jackpot engines; ThrillTech's announcement positions it as an early mover in the side-bet category specifically.

What Operators Will Likely Want to See Before Integration

Ontario operators evaluating ThrillPots will weigh three factors beyond the AGCO registration: (1) integration overhead — ThrillTech already ships pre-built connectors for major PAM platforms including Pragmatic Solutions, so operators on those stacks face minimal lift; (2) responsible-gaming alignment — the side-bet mechanic sits on top of the player's base wager, and operators will need to ensure the side-stake amount is included in any deposit-limit or session-timer tooling they have configured; and (3) AGCO's game-event approval — supplier registration is necessary but not sufficient; each operator-integrated deployment requires a separate game-event filing with iGaming Ontario before it can go live to Ontario players.

The game-event step typically adds 6-10 weeks to a launch timeline once an operator has signed a commercial agreement with ThrillTech. Realistically, the earliest an Ontario player could see a ThrillPots-enabled slot in the wild is late Q3 or early Q4 2026.

Context: Ontario's 2026 Supplier Wave

ThrillTech's licence is the latest in a steady stream of B2B supplier approvals in Ontario during 2026. Evoplay, Random State, Fennica Gaming, Playnetic, Gaming Corps, ELK Studios, ODDSworks, St8, TaDa Gaming, and edict egaming have all publicly disclosed AGCO supplier registration this year. The pace has accelerated since Alberta's regulated iGaming market opened on July 13, 2026, with several suppliers securing dual Ontario-Alberta approvals to position themselves for cross-province distribution from day one. Whether ThrillTech follows the same dual-registration pattern is not yet clear from the company's announcement.

For Ontario players, the practical upshot of the supplier wave is more choice at the slot-lobby level: more titles, more mechanics, and now a side-bet jackpot layer that wasn't available through any AGCO-licensed supplier before this month.

Responsible-Gaming Note

Side-bet jackpots in Ontario remain subject to the same player-protection framework as the base games they sit on top of. The AGCO's Standards for Internet Gaming require that any side wager be clearly disclosed at the bet-confirmation step, that it be included in the player's deposit-limit calculations if the operator has opted to apply limits at the session level, and that BetGuard self-exclusion block access to both the base game and any associated side bet. Operators integrating ThrillPots will be expected to honour those requirements from day one; the AGCO's enforcement record in 2026 (including the recent $170,000 monetary penalty against Great Canadian Entertainment) signals that side-mechanic compliance is an active supervision area, not a passive one.

Players who want to set a deposit limit or self-exclude from Ontario-regulated iGaming entirely can do so through BetGuard, the centralized self-exclusion tool that launched across the province in May 2026.
