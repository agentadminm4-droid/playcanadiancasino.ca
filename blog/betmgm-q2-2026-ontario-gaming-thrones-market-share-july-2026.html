BetMGM Q2 2026: Game of Thrones Exclusive Drives Ontario iGaming Growth

July 28, 2026 • 7 min read • By PlayCanadianCasino Team

On July 28, 2026, BetMGM — the 50/50 online betting venture between MGM Resorts International (NYSE: MGM) and Entain plc (LSE: ENT) — published its Q2 2026 business update, confirming that new Game of Thrones exclusive titles launched in Ontario are "amongst our most successful launches" for the quarter. The Ontario-exclusive release — a multi-title slate built around the HBO brand under BetMGM's existing partnership with Blueprint Gaming — is one of two named title families singled out in the company's operating-highlights section as a Q2 growth driver, alongside the company's broader exclusive-content pipeline of Rakin' Bacon, Buffalo Triple Power, and Money Gong.

The Ontario angle matters because BetMGM is the only AGCO-licensed operator in Canada carrying the Game of Thrones slot brand. Per the company's own disclosure, the Ontario exclusivity is staged to roll out in the United States later this summer — a sequence that places Ontario in the lead-market role for the franchise's commercial performance and makes Q2 2026 the first earnings window in which the Ontario-only deployment has measurable run-rate behind it.

What's in the BetMGM Q2 2026 Update for Ontario Players

The Q2 2026 update lands as the first formal earnings disclosure to confirm that the Ontario-only Game of Thrones deployment is producing commercially meaningful results for BetMGM. The studio behind the games, Blueprint Gaming, signed an exclusive partnership with BetMGM Ontario in April 2026 that gave the operator the only path to the Game of Thrones slot brand in Canada through the duration of the deal. The exclusivity window is structured so that Ontario receives the rollout first, with US-state distribution planned for "later this summer" per BetMGM's Q2 commentary — a sequencing choice that positions Ontario as the lead market for the franchise's commercial performance.

BetMGM's operating-highlights section names the Ontario Game of Thrones titles directly. "New Game of Thrones titles launched in Ontario amongst our most successful launches, with a planned US rollout this summer," the company wrote, alongside named reference to exclusive Rakin' Bacon, Buffalo Triple Power, and Money Gong franchise releases and the launch of Elvis Presley: Viva Las Records and Marilyn Monroe Slingo as the first two entries in a Hollywood-legends slot series. The commentary frames the Ontario-first releases as evidence that BetMGM's exclusive-content strategy is producing a measurable Q2 lift in player engagement metrics, with H1 net gaming revenue (NGR) per active up 9% year-over-year across the multi-product-state footprint.

Ontario iGaming Market Share: 20% in May 2026

Buried in the disclosure footnotes of the Q2 update is one of the most-cited data points for any Ontario-market earnings window: BetMGM's iGaming market share in the province. The note accompanying the financial summary specifies that the 20% iGaming share figure is based on "May 2026 for Ontario" operator-level data, with internal estimates used where operator-specific results are unavailable. The same footnote cites 8% Online Sports market share in Ontario and 13% combined GGR share across BetMGM's active North American footprint — the latter covering five iGaming and online-sports-betting markets and 25 sports-betting-only markets.

For Ontario context, the 20% iGaming share figure sits inside a market that closed fiscal year 2025–26 at CAD $103 billion in total wagers and $4.2 billion in gross gaming revenue, with channelization reaching 91.1% per the Ipsos year-four study cited by iGaming Ontario. A 20% share of that wager pool positions BetMGM Ontario as one of two or three operators carrying the bulk of AGCO-licensed iGaming handle in the province, alongside the long-standing private-operator cohort of Betway, LeoVegas, Hard Rock Bet, theScore, and the rest of the 45-operator roster.

Why Ontario-First Matters for the Q2 Story

The Ontario-first sequencing is a meaningful business decision that has emerged as a recurring pattern across BetMGM's North American exclusive-content pipeline. The April 2026 Blueprint Gaming partnership — the deal that put the Game of Thrones slot behind BetMGM Ontario's lobby and only BetMGM Ontario's lobby — was structured with a defined exclusivity window, but the financial upside of leading the launch in a smaller market (Ontario has roughly one-thirtieth the population of the combined US iGaming + sports-betting footprint) only makes sense if BetMGM is using Ontario to validate the title before committing to a larger-scale US rollout.

Q2 2026 is the first earnings cycle in which that validation is visible. BetMGM's "best in class iGaming" framing — which leads the operating-highlights section — is built around the company's exclusive-content slate, and the Ontario Game of Thrones deployment is the most prominent named example in the disclosure. The implied read for Ontario players is that the studio is betting on the province as a controlled-environment launch market, and that the slot's commercial performance here will determine how aggressively the US-state rollout proceeds later this summer.

The H2 outlook section of the Q2 release reinforces this. The company names "a strong World Cup," the Alberta launch, and Borgata's brand refresh as the three principal H2 growth inputs, with Alberta specifically cited as a "recent successful launch." The Alberta mention is notable because Alberta's regulated iGaming market only opened on July 13, 2026, meaning the Q2 2026 disclosure covers the first two weeks of the Alberta market — and BetMGM is signalling that its platform is already converting Alberta player acquisition into a measurable Q2 lift.

FY 2026 Guidance and the Lower-End Signal

BetMGM reaffirmed its FY 2026 guidance of $2.9–$3.1 billion in net revenue and $300–$350 million in Adjusted EBITDA, but flagged that delivery is tracking to the lower end of both ranges — a softer tone than the company's Q1 commentary, which had targeted the mid-point. The earnings release attributes the lower-end read to "year-to-date performance" rather than a fundamental strategy reset, but the timing is notable for an Ontario-market observer: the Q2 2026 cycle is the first in which the Ontario Game of Thrones deployment is fully reflected in the numbers.

The company also pushed out its $500 million Adjusted EBITDA milestone, which had previously been targeted for 2027. "BetMGM remains confident in delivering Adjusted EBITDA of $500 million in the coming years. However, given the current market environment including impact of prediction market regulatory complexity, we believe it is prudent to assume the timing of delivery will extend beyond current 2027 expectations." The reference to "prediction market regulatory complexity" is the first time BetMGM has named prediction-market operators as a competitive factor in its guidance commentary — a noteworthy signal given the recent launches of CIRO-authorized prediction-market products in Canada and the ongoing Kalshi/Polymarket activity in the United States.

What This Means for Ontario's AGCO-Licensed Operator Cohort

The Ontario competitive backdrop behind the BetMGM Q2 update is a market in which 45 AGCO-licensed private operators compete for roughly $9 billion in monthly iGaming handle — a figure that has held above $8 billion since November 2024 per iGaming Ontario's monthly performance reports. A 20% iGaming market share for BetMGM Ontario places the operator in the top tier of that competitive cohort, alongside Betway, LeoVegas, Hard Rock Bet, and theScore, which together with BetMGM are widely understood to carry the majority of AGCO-licensed iGaming handle in the province.

The disclosure is also notable for what it does not include. BetMGM did not break out Ontario-specific revenue figures in the Q2 release; the 20% market share figure is a separate disclosure based on operator-level tracking, not a directly reported financial line. For analysts who track the Ontario market by operator, the next data point will be the iGaming Ontario monthly performance report for May–June 2026, which will give the first independent operator-level cross-check against BetMGM's internal estimate.
