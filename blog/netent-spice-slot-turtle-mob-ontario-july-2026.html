How the Avalanche Grid and Feature Lane Work

Spice is built on a 6-reel, 5-row layout with a scatter-pays payout structure. According to GamblingNews, when 8 or more matching symbols land anywhere in view, a winning combination is formed. The Avalanche feature removes winning symbols, causing others to drop down to fill the grid, and Avalanches continue until no new winning combinations appear. The math model is high volatility with a top RTP of 96.09%, and stakes range from $/€0.20 to $/€60 per spin.

The Feature Lane sits above the grid and is the control panel for everything that elevates a base-game spin from a passive cascade into a swing-heavy outcome. The lane features a sequence of modifier symbols that fire together whenever a non-winning Avalanche takes place. The modifiers are Bombs, Random Wilds, Locked Wilds, the Row Converter, and the 9-Square Converter. The combination of all five modifiers on a single losing spin is what pushes the volatility into the high band — even a spin that looks dead at the start of the Avalanche can end with a feature-driven win.

The Feature Lane is consistent with NetEnt's recent push toward modifier-heavy grid slots — the same template that powered other recent releases in the studio's catalogue. The difference in Spice is that the modifiers are stacked horizontally in a single lane rather than scattered across the reel-set, which makes the modifier order visually predictable and helps players track which features are queued next.

The Bomb Symbol and the Converter Modifiers

The Bomb is the headline modifier and the one that defines Spice's swing profile. According to VegasSlotsOnline, when a Bomb lands during a non-winning Avalanche, it explodes and removes all symbols in its blast radius, then doubles the multiplier of every affected position. That design pushes variance upward by rewarding failure states — a feature rare in modern high-volatility slots, where most modifiers are gated by winning combinations rather than by losing ones.

The two converter modifiers are companion tools that reinforce the swing-heavy profile. The Row Converter triggers when no win is scored and no other modifier activates. It converts all symbols across a single row into copies of the same symbol, creating a near-miss state that often cascades into a winning Avalanche on the next drop. The 9-Square Converter applies the same logic to a 3×3 area instead of a single row, which is the higher-impact version of the same mechanic.

GamblingNews frames the modifier stack as NetEnt's answer to a crowded grid-slot market: "For NetEnt, the theme is less about narrative than positioning. Offbeat concepts help a legacy studio stand out in a market where mechanic families often blur together, and the turtle-mafia angle gives Spice an identity distinct from the crime-slot pack." The Bomb symbol and the two converters are the mechanic version of that positioning — they slot Spice into a distinct identity even among the broad modifier-grid category.

Free Spins, Super Free Spins, and the Feature Lane Bonus

Spice ships with two distinct bonus rounds, both tied to the Feature Lane mechanic. The Free Spins round offers up to 3 spins, with multiplier frames that double on every consecutive win. A Super Free Spins variant assigns random multipliers to every position on the grid from the start of the round, raising the ceiling on a single bonus trigger. The differing volatility between the two rounds gives NetEnt a clean tier for player preferences — Free Spins is the controlled-volatility tier, Super Free Spins is the maximum-upside option.

The Feature Lane bonus is the structural evolution that distinguishes Spice from older NetEnt grid slots. In those earlier releases, the modifier icons were scattered across the top of the grid and triggered in sequence; in Spice, the Feature Lane is a single horizontal strip that ties all five modifiers together into a shared mechanic stack. According to iGaming-Business-Hub, that design "activates on losing Avalanches and creates the opportunity to convert a dead spin into a recovery spin, which is the structural engine that pushes Spice into the high-volatility band."

The 15,000x max win is the headline number for the release. According to the Bigwinboard coverage and NetEnt's launch data, Spice's win cap is reachable inside the Free Spins round where the Feature Lane modifiers stack on top of consecutive Avalanche multipliers. The Avalanche foundation is the same mechanic NetEnt has used across its recent grid portfolio, but the layered modifiers and the Free Spins multiplier frames are what give Spice its own identity in the category.
