NetEnt Reopens the Iconic Fruit Shop — Grand Re-Opening Brings a 36-Step Flavor Meter and 5,000x Max Win

July 2, 2026 • 5 min read • By PlayCanadianCasino Team

NetEnt's most recognizable fruit stall reopened its shutters on July 2, 2026 with the global launch of Fruit Shop Grand Re-Opening, a sequel to the 2013 original that defined the studio's early signature style. According to NetEnt's game page, the release date is set at July 2, 2026, and the title is rolling out across the studio's worldwide distribution network on the same day. NetEnt is part of Evolution's game studio group and the studio's content is widely deployed at AGCO-licensed Ontario operators, so the slot should land in most provincial lobbies in the days following the global release.

The structural story for Ontario players is the math model — not the fruit. Fruit Shop Grand Re-Opening runs on a 5x3 reel layout with 34 paylines, but the grid can expand to 5x6 during the bonus round via Extra Row upgrades. The headline mechanic is a 36-step Flavor Meter that fills on every winning spin, persistent multipliers of up to 14x on Re-Spins, sticky wilds on random reel positions, and a max win of 5,000x the stake. The RTP at the standard 96% operator tier sits at 96.37%, volatility is rated High, and bets span C$0.20 to C$100.

The Flavor Meter and the Re-Spin Engine

The defining feature of Fruit Shop Grand Re-Opening is the Flavor Meter, a 36-step progression that builds as players land winning combinations. On any base-game win, the reels Re-Spin while the winning combination stays locked, and the meter advances a step for each new win during the chain — combining both the win payout and the meter progress into a single feedback loop. Sticky Wilds can land on random reel positions during a Re-Spin chain, and any active multiplier applies on top of the original win before the spin resolves.

The math difference from the original 2013 Fruit Shop is meaningful. Where the original's free spins rounds were short and triggered frequently on fruit combinations, the new title layers a persistent meter across the entire bonus sequence — a chain of Re-Spins that hits multiple flavor steps can compound a single bet into a much larger return before the round ends. The grid expansion is a structural reset too: when the meter triggers an Extra Row upgrade, the layout grows from 5x3 to 5x6, increasing the active paylines and the hit potential for the rest of the bonus sequence. According to FruitySlots' review, the meter builds consistently rather than stalling — the game rarely feels like it goes cold between significant events.

Free Spins, Multipliers, and the Expanding Grid

The free spins round triggers when three or more Cherry scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. During the bonus round, the Flavor Meter persists throughout the full feature rather than resetting between spins, so a meter that begins at step 12 can still be at step 12 ten free spins later — the additive progression compounds across the entire bonus sequence rather than each individual spin. Multipliers during the bonus round can reach up to 14x on a single Re-Spin, applied to the locked winning combination before the spin payout resolves.

Beyond the bonus trigger, Fruit Shop Grand Re-Opening introduces Extra Row upgrades as a separate reward mechanic. When the meter crosses certain thresholds during the bonus round, an extra row expands the grid from 5x3 to 5x6, increasing the active paylines for the remainder of the bonus sequence. The classic Fruit Shop symbols — cherries, lemons, oranges, plums, watermelons — return as the high-paying premium cast, with the cherry functioning both as the scatter trigger and the highest-paying base symbol. According to the game's official release notes, the friendly fruit-shop look is deliberately retained from the 2013 original while the math model and reel architecture are rebuilt from scratch.

RTP, Volatility, and the 5,000x Max Win

Fruit Shop Grand Re-Opening ships with an RTP of 96.37% at the standard 96% operator tier and a maximum win of 5,000x the stake, which at a C$1 bet equals roughly C$5,000 and at the typical C$100 top bet equals C$500,000. Reaching the ceiling typically requires running the Flavor Meter deep into the additive stack during a long bonus round with multiple Expanded Grid upgrades active simultaneously — it is a bonus-round outcome rather than a base-game event. The volatility rating is High, which is a step up from the original 2013 release's medium-low profile and reflects the persistent meter math model.

In selected markets and with participating operators, Fruit Shop Grand Re-Opening may feature a Buy Bonus system. Players can choose between standard Free Spins or a premium trigger package featuring expanded starting meter progress, with up to three Buy Bonus options available depending on jurisdiction. The Buy Bonus is one of the more interesting structural choices in the release because it lets players opt into the higher-variance meter configuration directly rather than waiting for the scatter to land organically — but Buy Bonus availability is jurisdiction-dependent and not every AGCO-licensed operator will offer it.
