What the Max Scatter Engine Actually Does

The slot wraps a familiar sweets aesthetic around a scatter-pays grid where wins form when eight or more matching symbols land anywhere on screen. Cascades then remove the winning symbols and drop new ones into place, continuing until no more winning combinations appear. The Max Scatter symbol sits alongside the regular scatter and is what separates this title from the wave of similar cascade slots on Ontario lobbies.

Rather than serving only as a free-spins trigger, Max Scatter symbols can award instant prizes from 100x when one lands alongside at least three regular scatters, scaling up to the full 15,000x maximum when four Max Scatters combine with four or more scatters. If the maximum win is paid through the Max Scatter route, the free spins round is forfeited rather than layered on top — a design choice that puts the entire top prize inside a single base-game combination rather than behind a multi-trigger bonus chain.

Free Spins, BOOST+ and the Bonus Buy Stack

When the bonus round does trigger, it follows a conventional cascade-multiplier structure: four or more scatter symbols (counting Max Scatters) award ten free spins, with random multipliers between 2x and 100x landing on special reels. Multiplier symbols remain in place until a cascade sequence ends, the values combine into a single total, and that total multiplies the chain's payout — a compounding structure that suits the high-volatility profile.

Players who want to shortcut the wait can use the studio's BOOST+ side bet, which raises the stake by 25% in exchange for a higher chance of triggering the bonus feature from the base game. The standard Bonus Buy costs 100x the bet and guarantees free spins, while the Super Buy costs 500x and seeds starting multipliers between 15x and 100x, which materially shortens the path to the slot's compounding-multiplier ceiling.
