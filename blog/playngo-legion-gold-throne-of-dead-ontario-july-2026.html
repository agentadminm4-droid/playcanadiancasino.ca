How the Legion Gold and the Dead Series Crossover Actually Works

Coin Respins, the Mega Spins Upgrade, and the Throne of Dead Picker

Coin Respins is the headline feature and triggers when the grid fills with Coin symbols and at least one Collect symbol lands in the same spin. The trigger is essentially the Legion Gold 2 trigger pattern — a full set of Coins plus the Collect — but the respin round itself has been reworked for this release. The base Coin Respin round starts with three respins and resets to three whenever a new Coin or Collect symbol lands on the reels. During the round, only Coin symbols and special symbols can land — premium pays, scatters, and wilds are stripped out — narrowing the action to the coin-collection core.

The Mega Spin upgrade is the higher-paying variant of Coin Respins and triggers randomly when a Trophy symbol lands during the base game. Mega Spin upgrades the Coin values to a higher denomination, materially increasing the ceiling of any single respin chain. The Throne of Dead picker is the second feature layer and triggers when three or more Throne of Dead scatters land anywhere on the reels; the picker game awards a coin chest bonus that is paid out as a fixed multiplier on the triggering stake. The combination of Coin Respins, Mega Spin upgrade, and the Throne of Dead picker gives the slot three distinct bonus pathways with different math profiles, which is more depth than the original Legion Gold 2 release shipped with — and is the clearest signal that the crossover format is being used as a vehicle to expand the underlying math, not just re-skin it.
