DAZN Bet Goes Live in Ontario With Sportsbook and iCasino on Launch Day

July 1, 2026 • 7 min read • By PlayCanadianCasino Team

DAZN Bet, the sports betting and iGaming arm of global sports streaming giant DAZN, went live in Ontario on Tuesday, June 30, 2026, making the province the operator's first North American market. The launch brings both an online sportsbook and an iCasino product to Ontario players under the AGCO and iGaming Ontario regulatory framework, alongside DAZN's existing Canadian streaming business.

The debut was announced in a press release on June 30. DAZN Bet is already live in several European jurisdictions; Ontario is the operator's first regulated North American market, and CEO Valery Gelfman framed the entry as a long-term commitment to the Canadian market.

Key Facts

  • Launched: Tuesday, June 30, 2026 — DAZN Bet went live in Ontario with online sports betting and iGaming under AGCO and iGaming Ontario oversight.

  • Streaming angle: DAZN holds broadcast rights to NFL Sunday Ticket in Canada and other major sports, which the operator intends to leverage for the sportsbook product.
  • Alberta footprint: DAZN Bet is registered with the Alberta sports betting regulator and is in line to launch in that province as early as July 13, 2026, the opening day of Alberta's competitive iGaming market.
