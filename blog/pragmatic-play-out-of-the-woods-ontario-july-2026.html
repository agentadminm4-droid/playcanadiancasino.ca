How the Stacked Multiplier Wild Engine Works

The mechanical core is the Multiplier Wild stack — a full stacked reel of wild symbols where each position carries an increasing multiplier, starting at 1x on the bottom symbol and rising to 4x at the top. The stack can only appear as a full reel, which means it lands as a vertical column of up to four wild multipliers when it triggers. When the stack contributes to a winning combination, a respin is awarded — the symbols move down one or more positions, revealing new Multiplier Wilds above the stack, and each newly revealed symbol increases in value. The mechanic is a clear evolution of Pragmatic's cascading-wild formula (used in Fury of the Gods and several earlier releases) but with the multiplier-climb layer added on top — successive respins don't just refresh the grid, they actively grow the multiplier values.

The gold-rimmed Multiplier Wild is the engine's higher-paying variant. If a gold-rimmed Multiplier Wild reaches the bottom row of the reel and another respin is awarded, the stack moves down by between 2 and 10 positions — creating a much longer respin chain than the base mechanic and allowing the multiplier values to climb into the higher tiers. Per the studio's game page, the gold-rimmed variant can compound substantially during a long respin sequence, particularly when combined with multiple multiplier wild stacks on adjacent reels. That compounding potential is the slot's route to the 10,000x top ceiling and is the structural reason the engine has a 10,000x cap rather than the 5,000x cap that some of the studio's earlier medium-volatility releases shipped with.
