Play'n GO Releases Temple of Three With Three Egyptian God Pot Features

July 29, 2026 • 5 min read • By PlayCanadianCasino Team

Play'n GO released Temple of Three on July 28, 2026, a 3-reel Egyptian slot built around the Treasure Keeper mechanic and three god-themed Pot features. The release positions Ra, Bastet and Anubis as distinct modifiers on a Hold'n Spin framework, with GO Ultra sitting alongside them as an enhancement mode for the Cash Coin and Instant Prize tiers.

The release matters for Ontario because Play'n GO is an established supplier at AGCO-licensed operators, including BetMGM Ontario and LeoVegas Ontario. A network-wide launch through the studio's regular distribution means the title is positioned to appear across Ontario's regulated slot catalogue, although individual lobby timing is set by each operator.

Key Facts: Temple of Three

  • First-party publication date: July 28, 2026
  • Provider: Play'n GO, an established supplier at AGCO-licensed Ontario operators
  • Theme and format: 3-reel Egyptian slot, 5 paylines, wins left-to-right on adjacent reels
  • Hero mechanic: Treasure Keeper on the middle reel triggers Divine Re-spins and collects Cash Coin symbols
  • Pot features: Bastet's Multiplier Pot, Ra's Mystery Pot, Anubis' Enhancer Pot — any combination can trigger together
  • Bonus mode: Hold'n Spin with Second Blessing, plus GO Ultra for boosted Cash Coin and Instant Prize tiers
  • Category: Studio release, available to Play'n GO operators

Source: Play'n GO newsroom release, July 28, 2026 · Last updated: July 29, 2026

How the Three Pot Features Connect to Hold'n Spin

According to the studio's release, the hero mechanic is a Hold'n Spin framework built around three Pot features, each tied to one of the Egyptian gods named in the title. Bastet's Multiplier Pot adds multiplier frames to the grid, Ra's Mystery Pot introduces a sticky Mystery symbol that reveals an Instant Prize at the end of Hold'n Spin, and Anubis' Enhancer Pot raises the value tier of Cash Coin symbols. One, two, or all three Pot features can trigger together, which lets a single feature sequence combine a multiplier frame, a sticky Mystery reveal, and an enhanced Cash Coin value at the same time.

Activating at least one Pot feature triggers Hold'n Spin, where qualifying symbols remain in place while the rest of the reels spin again. Landing any symbol resets the re-spins, and Second Blessing can add a Cash Coin symbol on the final re-spin and continue the feature. The combination of multiple modifier Pots plus Hold'n Spin re-triggers is what gives the engine its main payout pathway, and it is the structural reason the release leans on a 3-reel format rather than the studio's usual grid slots.

The Treasure Keeper and Divine Re-spins

The Treasure Keeper symbol is the on-reel anchor for the bonus. It can land on the middle reel to trigger Divine Re-spins and start collecting Cash Coin symbols. The Treasure Keeper moves down the reel with each re-spin, while additional Treasure Keepers can re-trigger the feature and reset the re-spins, which gives the mechanic a clear sense of vertical movement and escalation across the re-spin cycle.

The Treasure Keeper plus the Pot features is the structural core of the release. The 3-reel format keeps the layout readable while the modifier stack keeps the feature set deep, which is a different balance than Play'n GO has struck on its grid-based titles like Reactoonz Blitzways. The studio is using the simpler reel structure as a counterweight to a feature set that can branch in several directions during a single bonus round.

GO Ultra and Where Temple of Three Fits in Play'n GO's Slate

Temple of Three includes GO Ultra, which enhances Cash Coin symbol values and Instant Prize tiers when active. GO Ultra is positioned as a way to upgrade the Hold'n Spin ceiling on a per-session basis rather than a separate bonus game, and it pairs naturally with the Anubis' Enhancer Pot when both are live in the same sequence.

The release sits inside Play'n GO's broader pattern of feature-led 3-reel and 5-reel titles that pair an Egyptian or adventure theme with a Hold'n Spin engine. Earlier in July the studio published Reactoonz Blitzways on July 23 and Fate's Fortune on July 16, and Temple of Three continues the summer cadence with a more compact reel format. The value for Ontario players is in the modifier stack (Bastet, Ra, and Anubis working together) and the GO Ultra enhancement, not in a novel payline structure.
