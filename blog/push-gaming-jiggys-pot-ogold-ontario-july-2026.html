A Compact Cash-Collect Slot Built Around the Single Centre Position

Jiggy's Pot O'Gold runs on a 3x3 reel layout with 5 fixed paylines, an unusual configuration for Push Gaming's recent catalogue — most 2026 Push Gaming releases have leaned into 6x4 or larger grids with mechanic-heavy bonus rounds. The 3x3 design is deliberate, according to trade-press coverage of the release: Reel Hot Games, the sub-studio that shipped the title, has been positioning itself as Push Gaming's "feature-focused, compact release" label. The previous Reel Hot Games title — Vegas Vault, released earlier in 2026 — used the same 3x3 frame and a similar cash-collect mechanic, but with a Vegas vault-crasher theme rather than the Irish-pot-of-gold packaging here.

The defining structural element is the centre position. When the Bonus symbol lands on the central reel (reel 2, row 2), it triggers the Instant Prize free spins round. The bonus symbol acts as a trigger only on the centre position; on any other position, it pays as a regular symbol. That single-position trigger is uncommon in modern slot design and creates a recognisable visual cue for players: seeing the Bonus on reel 2 and any other position is a free-spins entry, while the same symbol scattered elsewhere is just another paying symbol. The design choice simplifies the bonus trigger visually without weakening the math — the Instant Prize bonus round carries the slot's main payout potential, and the centre-trigger requirement is what keeps the base game comfortably low in volatility.

Instant Prize Free Spins, Cash Booster, and the Mighty Craic Upgrade

The free spins round triggers when the Bonus symbol lands on the centre position, awarding four free spins as the base. During the bonus, only prize symbols (cash values), Collectors, and additional Bonus symbols land on the reels — wilds and most other base-game symbols are stripped out, narrowing the action to the cash-collection core. The Cash Booster feature awards an additional free spin every time a Collector symbol lands, building the bonus round into a chain retrigger pattern, and the boosters stack without a hard cap during the free spins round.

The Mighty Craic Free Spins mode is the slot's high-end variant and triggers randomly when the Instant Prize and Jackpot symbols have filled the Pot O'Gold meter above the reels during the base game. Mighty Craic awards six free spins and introduces a sticky Collector on the reels for the duration of the round, which materially changes the cash-collection math: instead of Collectors appearing and disappearing spin-by-spin, each Collector locks in place and continues accumulating from subsequent Prize symbols for the entire bonus. The sticky Collector mechanic is what makes the Mighty Craic round the slot's theoretical route to the 1,000x fixed-jackpot ceiling — at high end, multiple Sticky Collectors and a steady stream of Jackpot symbols in the same round can compound into payout sequences that the four-spin Instant Prize round cannot match.
