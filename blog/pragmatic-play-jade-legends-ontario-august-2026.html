How the 7×7 Cluster Grid and Position-Specific Coin Multipliers Work

Jade Legends is built on a 7-reel, 7-row layout with a cluster-pays payout structure. According to slots.info's per-slot release data, when 5 or more matching symbols land adjacently anywhere in view, a winning combination is formed. The Tumble mechanic removes winning clusters from the grid, causing the remaining symbols to drop down with new symbols filling in from the top, and tumbles continue until no new clusters appear. The math model is high volatility (per slotark's release notes) with a top RTP of 96.50%, and stakes range from $/€0.20 to $/€240 per spin.

The headline mechanic is Coin Collect with position-specific multipliers. Gold-marked grid positions persist across tumbles within a single round and grow their multiplier value with every cascade — a coin collector symbol that lands on a high-mult gold square pays more than the same coin would on a low-mult square. As slotark's review notes, "where a coin lands changes what it is worth," which is the real differentiation versus the flat multiplier systems Pragmatic Play has shipped elsewhere in its scatter-pays family.

The base game also includes a secondary set of standard Multiplier symbols that contribute to the total multiplier applied to cluster wins in the same manner as the studio's other scatter-pays titles, with the gold-position system sitting on top of (not replacing) that mechanic. According to PragmaticSpins' release notes, the visual identity centres on a "golden temple garden" motif, with a white-bearded sage character and a small dragon coiled around one arm. The release-day slot art uses jade green, river-stone grey, and warm gold as the dominant palette, with red lantern and folding-fan accents.

The Four Free-Spin Tiers: Standard, Enhanced, Super, and Ultra

Jade Legends ships with four free-spin bonus tiers, layered in increasing order of feature intensity, according to slotark's release data:

  • Standard Free Spins: The base-tier bonus. Free-spin rounds trigger on the scatter threshold, and the gold-marked multiplier positions carry over into the round with their accumulated value.
  • Enhanced Free Spins: Adds a higher base frequency of gold-marked positions during the round. The position-specific multiplier growth continues across tumbles, but the round starts with more multi-marked squares than Standard.
  • Super Free Spins: The first tier where the Coin Collect payout is meaningfully boosted and gold-marked positions reset to higher starting multipliers at the start of the round.
  • Ultra Free Spins: The top tier and the only one where the 20,000x max win is realistically reachable, according to slotark's 8.5/10 review. The Coin Collect mechanic runs at full intensity and the gold-marked position growth is unbounded within the round.

According to slotark's analysis, the four-tier structure gives Jade Legends real bonus-cycle depth rather than nominal label variations. Standard is the safest ride and the most frequent trigger; Enhanced adds a position-frequency boost; Super resets the position multipliers upward; Ultra is the full-upside configuration. The choice of which bonus lands is dictated by the scatter combination that triggers the round (or by the player's choice at the Bonus Buy screen), not by random tier rolling.

Bonus Buy Ladder and the 20,000x Max Win

Jade Legends ships with a three-step Bonus Buy ladder, per slotark's release data: 100x the stake for the Standard Free Spins, 250x for the Super Free Spins tier, and 750x for the Ultra Free Spins tier. The Enhanced tier is not exposed as a direct buy option; it functions as a mid-game transition between Standard and Super during natural play.

The 20,000x max win is the headline number for the release. According to slotark's per-slot data, Jade Legends ties Death Dominion's release-week cap and lands at the top of Pragmatic Play's 2026 scatter-pays family (Gates of Olympus remains at 5,000x; the studio has been steadily raising the ceiling across the family). The 96.50% top RTP is competitive for a high-volatility cluster release, and the $240 maximum bet combined with the 20,000x ceiling produces a theoretical top dollar payout that qualifies as serious high-stakes territory.

Slotark's review credits Jade Legends with being Pragmatic Play's most mechanically sophisticated cluster slot to date, scoring 8.5/10. The reservation in the score is that the coin mechanic, while strong in principle, can produce outcomes that feel driven more by coin placement than by accumulated round state — individual coin landings matter more than the gradual build the player experiences from the cascade chain. That's a fair criticism, but it does not change the ceiling calculation: the 20,000x is reachable in Ultra Free Spins, and the position-specific multiplier system is genuinely differentiated from anything Pragmatic Play has shipped in the cluster family so far.
