## Strategy in 5 lines

1. **Tier 1 first** — High-DA Canadian/Ontario-focused targets. They have the most SEO value AND the highest topical relevance. One link from Casino.org/CA (DA 70) is worth 50 directory links.
2. **Don't spam directories** — Google has been devaluing generic directory links since 2024. 1-2 quality links/week beats 50 junk links.
3. **Guest posts > submissions** — A guest post on Canadian Gaming Business (DA 55, in-text link) beats a Casinomeister forum signature 10x.
4. **Trade pubs first, affiliates second** — Trade pubs (iGamingBusiness.com, GamblingNews.com) are where journalists source their stats. If they cite your article once, every future story that mentions "91% channelization" links to you.
5. **NO self-promo on Reddit/forums** — Get banned instantly. Build karma for 60+ days first, then put the link in your user profile (not the post).

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Spamming 50 directories in one day** — looks like a link scheme, Google devalues it. Max 2-3 submissions per day.
2. **Generic copy-paste submissions** — every directory has a "describe your site" field. Customise the 155-char description for each one.
3. **Submitting to .ca-only directories from a non-Canadian IP** — some directories check. If you're using a VPN, turn it off.
4. **Paying for directory listings** — never. Google explicitly devalues paid directory links.
5. **Posting affiliate links in Reddit/forum comments** — instant ban. Build karma first.
6. **Forgetting to update the tracking spreadsheet** — if you don't track, you'll re-submit the same site twice and waste a contact form attempt.

## When to escalate

If after 30 days you've sent 30+ outreach emails and gotten zero guest post approvals, the problem is your pitch, not the targets. Re-read Template 1 in `outreach-templates.md` and:
- Make the subject line more specific
- Mention 2-3 specific articles they've published (proves you read the site)
- Drop the offer of "one per month as a recurring series" (too committal for a first email)
- Keep it to 200 words max

If after 60 days you have 5+ guest posts live AND a 3.0+ referring domain growth in Ahrefs/SEMrush, you're in great shape. **That's the threshold where SEO starts compounding.**
