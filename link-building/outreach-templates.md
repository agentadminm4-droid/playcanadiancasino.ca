## Best Practices

### DO
- **Personalize every email** — reference a specific article they wrote. Generic templates get deleted in 3 seconds.
- **Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am recipient's local time** — best open rates
- **Use a real name in the From field** (James Mitchell, not "The PlayCanadianCasino.ca Team")
- **Keep subject lines under 50 characters** — long ones get cut off on mobile
- **Follow up 2x max** — then stop. Three emails = harassment.
- **Track every send in a spreadsheet** — date sent, response, follow-up date, outcome
- **Use a tracking column for "link acquired?"** — Y/N/pending

### DON'T
- ❌ Don't CC multiple sites on the same pitch — looks like spam
- ❌ Don't send from "noreply@" addresses — looks like bulk email
- ❌ Don't ask for "link exchanges" — Google penalizes this
- ❌ Don't pay for links — Google penalizes this too
- ❌ Don't pitch "write for us" sites that accept everything — those have spammy link profiles
- ❌ Don't include attachments on first contact — many email clients strip them
- ❌ Don't use exclamation points in subject lines (!!!) — screams spam
- ❌ Don't send the same email twice to the same person — check your sent folder
