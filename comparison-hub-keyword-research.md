## Compliance reminders (AGCO C-2.04)

These pages are HIGH commercial intent. The temptation will be to write "Betway is the best for payouts!" — but AGCO doesn't allow comparative "best" claims without substantiation. The safer framing:

✅ "Based on [data source], [Casino X] advertises faster payout processing times than [Casino Y]"
✅ "Royal Vegas's Microgaming library includes 800+ slots; Jackpot City's library is 500+"
✅ "Both casinos are AGCO-licensed and pass our 25-point review process"

❌ "Royal Vegas is the best casino in Ontario"
❌ "Jackpot City has the fastest payouts in Canada" (unsubstantiated superlative)
❌ "Sign up with Royal Vegas using promo code X and get 100 free spins" (no promo language per C-2.04)

Each page needs a "Last reviewed: [date]" stamp and the standard footer.
