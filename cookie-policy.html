What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners. This Cookie Policy explains the specific cookies we use on PlayCanadianCasino.ca and how you can control them.

On your first visit, a cookie-consent banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for our site — the banner will reappear on your next visit.

Most browsers let you refuse all cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you before a cookie is stored. Refer to your browser's help documentation:
